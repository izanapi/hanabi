HANABI

C

2 BARS

Settings

Strings. Swipe to play. Left is lower, right is higher. Faster swipes play louder; the outer edge sounds brighter.

Inner strings. Curl into the white crown to add a perfect fifth (+7 semitones). Move deeper to add an octave (+12) as well. The white tips breathe; their visible length is the harmonic boundary. The center is silent.

Modes. CHORD adds left-hand harmony. ARP repeats held notes as arpeggios. Shift + a note key adds a fifth; Alt adds a fifth and an octave.

Loop. Press REC, then play. Two bars repeat automatically. PAUSE keeps the phrase; CLEAR erases it. Key and scale changes transpose the loop, including inner harmonics.

Dice. Randomizes key, scale, voice and color. Tempo, mix and your loop stay unchanged.

Keys. A S D F G H J: bass. Q W E R T Y U: treble. Space: loop. Esc: stop.
